GAME GUIDES

Choose a game that fits your room and your group.

Practical introductions to popular indoor games, from competitive cue sports to quick social formats.

POOL & BILLIARDS

A classic game of angles and control.

Pool rewards repeat practice without demanding complicated rules at the start. A good setup needs cue clearance around every side and consistent lighting above the playing surface.

DARTS

Compact setup, serious depth.

Darts is easy to add to a limited space. Focus on a safe throwing lane, wall protection, consistent board height and a clearly marked oche.

TABLE TENNIS

Fast reactions and constant movement.

Beginners can start with basic drives and serves before exploring spin, placement and faster exchanges.
