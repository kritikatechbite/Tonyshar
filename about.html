ABOUT TONYSHARK

Independent indoor-game guidance without the noise.

TonyShark is an editorial project built around practical, understandable information for people exploring indoor games and game-room setups.

What we cover

We focus on indoor games that can be enjoyed at home, in clubs, social spaces and casual recreation venues.

How we write

Our goal is clarity rather than hype. We explain what a game is like to live with, what new players should know, and what questions are worth asking before buying equipment or dedicating room space.

Independent positioning

TonyShark is not an official representative of any game manufacturer, governing body, venue or retailer.
